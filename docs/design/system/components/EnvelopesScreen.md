# EnvelopesScreen

The Envelopes tab has two segments (Tabs): Envelopes and Reports. Splitting money and sharing a report live here; reading where money went is the Insights tab in the bottom bar.

- Envelopes: Unallocated with the Allocate button, then every envelope (swatch, name, balance, target Progress), "New envelope" in the header, reorder by holding a row, "Archived (n)". The envelope sheet (name, colour, optional target) and envelope detail are unchanged from the Screens section.
- Reports: the existing report builder in one Card (From, To, envelope Checkboxes, the "Include merchant and notes" Switch, Preview report), then the preview and Send steps as before. The Share report button in the Dashboard header jumps here. The last send's status shows under the card.
- Desktop 1280: the same two Tabs across the top of the page; Reports shows the builder on the left and the live preview and recent sends on the right. Envelopes becomes a table.
- Months before November and the "vs October" deltas are illustrative sample data; the sample ledger itself holds only November.
- States: no envelopes (Envelopes segment shows the start card; Reports says "Nothing to show yet"); over-allocation on Allocate; report preview loading Skeleton; send disabled until the preview is ready.
